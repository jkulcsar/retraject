retraject trajectory profile explorer

Three robot joints, each with its own move, limits, and interpolation law. Every law is a normalized shape r(τ) scaled by the move — the position, velocity, and acceleration curves below are the chain-rule images of that one function. With synchronize on, all joints are stretched to the slowest one (the 1997 Robot::SetUpTime); watch the faster joints' velocity and acceleration shrink while their paths keep their shape. Units are arbitrary but consistent (think degrees and seconds).
