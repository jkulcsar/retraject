retraject virtual stepper — the 1997 execution layer

In 1997 the computed trajectory had to leave the computer: an interrupt handler hooked to the PC timer chip pulsed step and direction bits through the parallel port to the RIP02's motors. The bridge was the Time Constant Array — per division of the trajectory, a countdown value: every TCA-th interrupt, one step. This layer never had a graphical representation; it lived as DumpOut's number columns on the DOS console and as debug characters the handler wrote straight into video memory. This page renders it for the first time — and the blue panel below reproduces the original console output, faithfully. Toggle 1997 countdown to compare the original one-constant-per-division timing (watch the error ripple) against the per-step timing the TIME00 prototype pointed toward.
