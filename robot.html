retraject kinematics explorer

The R6 arm: six revolute joints described by Denavit–Hartenberg parameters, with a spherical wrist (joints 4–6 intersect in one point — the property the upcoming inverse kinematics will exploit). The 3D scene graph mirrors the DH chain exactly, and the test suite proves it equals the forward kinematics to machine precision. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom; demo move runs a synchronized quintic trajectory from the trajectory module across all six joints — the first handshake between the two halves of this project.

Part of retraject — the DH convention, the arm's geometry, and the validation strategy live in src/kinematics/README.md.
