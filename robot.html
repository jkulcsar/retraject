retraject kinematics explorer

The R6 arm: six revolute joints described by Denavit–Hartenberg parameters, with a spherical wrist (joints 4–6 intersect in one point), which is what makes closed-form inverse kinematics possible. Drive it both ways: move the sliders (forward kinematics) or drag the gizmo (inverse kinematics — the analytic solver runs on every frame of the drag, picking the solution closest to the current pose, or pin one of the eight branches: shoulder front/back × elbow down/up × wrist flip). A red target means out of reach or outside joint limits — the robot holds, as a real controller would. Switch the gizmo to rotate to command orientation; demo move plays a synchronized quintic trajectory from the trajectory module through all six joints.

Part of retraject — the DH convention, the arm's geometry, and the validation strategy live in src/kinematics/README.md.
