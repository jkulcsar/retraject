retraject kinematics explorer

The R6 arm: six revolute joints from Denavit–Hartenberg parameters, with a spherical wrist — the property that makes closed-form inverse kinematics possible. Three ways to work with it, matching the three panel sections:

  1. Pose — the joint sliders are the robot (forward kinematics); the TCP readout follows.
  2. Target — drag the gizmo and the analytic IK follows on every frame, choosing the solution closest to the current pose (or pin one of the eight shoulder/elbow/wrist branches). A red target = out of reach or beyond joint limits; the robot holds, as a real controller would. Move straight to target executes a Cartesian line — with honest controller errors when the line can't be done.
  3. Program — teach waypoints like a pendant, pick a law per segment (or blend vias to pass through them without stopping), then play: the robot replays while the charts below scrub in sync. Demos: rest-to-rest path · blended vias.
